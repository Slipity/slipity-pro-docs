Every screen in Slipity Pro has a role:

- **Audience screens** show the service: lyrics, scripture, media and cameras.
- **Stage screens** show the people on the platform what's next, the time, notes and chords. See [Stage screens](/presenting/stage-screens).

Set them up in **Settings › Screens**.

## Projectors and TVs

Connect a display to your Mac or iPad and it appears in **Settings › Screens**. Choose it for a screen, and Slipity Pro remembers it by the display itself, so it comes back to the same job next time it's connected.

- **Identify Screens** puts a name on each display so you can tell them apart.
- **Test Pattern** helps line up a projector.
- **Corner Pin**, **Edge Blend** and **Alpha Key** correct a picture for the room: a projector at an angle, two projectors blended into one image, or a key for a video switcher.
- A display can be split into a **2 × 2 Quadrant** or used as part of a **TV Wall**.

On the Mac, **⌥⌘1** and **⌥⌘2** turn the audience and stage screens on and off. They work even when an output covers the screen you're working on.

## Apple TV

Install the free **Slipity Screen** app on an Apple TV on the same network.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the Apple TV">In **Settings › Screens**, choose the Apple TV as a screen's display.</Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">The Apple TV shows a code. Type it in to pair. Pairing lets this device drive it, and takes over from any Mac driving it now.</Step>
</Steps>

The Apple TV draws the slides itself, at its own refresh, so motion is as smooth as a projector, not a video of one.
