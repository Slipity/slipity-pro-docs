## Play and Edit

Slipity Pro has two modes, switched from the toolbar.

- **Play** is for running a service. A click or tap on a slide puts it on the screens straight away.
- **Edit** is for building one. A click picks a slide to work on without sending anything to the screens.

## Put a slide up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sample Service">
    Choose **Sample Service** in the sidebar, then **Amazing Grace** in the running order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a slide">
    In Play, the slide goes live. It is outlined in yellow, and the preview shows what the screens show.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move on">
    Press the arrow keys, or click the next slide. At the end of a presentation, the next one in the running order follows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Picking slides without showing them

Hold **Shift** or **Command** while clicking to pick several slides in blue without putting any of them up. Right-click (or long-press on iPad and iPhone) for everything you can do to them: groups, labels, actions, copy and paste. See [The running order](/presenting/running-order).
