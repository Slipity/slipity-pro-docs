## The window

| Part | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Toolbar** | Play and Edit, Settings, and the panels: Library, Flow, Media Library, Audio, Search, Themes, and the Voice Follow buttons. |
| **Sidebar** | Your libraries, where presentations are kept, and your playlists, which put them in the order of a service. |
| **Slides** | The slides of whatever is picked, as a grid or a list. In a playlist, every presentation in the service, one after another. |
| **Preview** | Exactly what the audience screens show, with the clear buttons and the audio meter. |
| **Panels** | Media, Audio, Flow and Search slide in beside or below the slides when you open them. |

On iPad and iPhone the same parts are arranged for the screen. See [iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo](/getting-started/iphone-and-ipad).

## Play and Edit

Slipity Pro has two modes, switched with the two buttons at the start of the toolbar.

- **Play** (Present Mode) runs a service. A click or tap on a slide puts it on the screens straight away.
- **Edit** builds one. A click picks a slide to work on without sending anything to the screens, and the slide editor opens beside it.

Switching to Edit while a slide is up leaves the screens alone: the audience keeps the slide they're looking at until you go back to Play and choose another.

## Put a slide up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sample Service">
    Choose **Sample Service** in the sidebar. Its presentations appear in the slides, one after another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a slide">
    In Play, the slide goes live. It's outlined in yellow, and the preview shows what the screens show.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move on">
    Press **→**, click the next slide, or tap the preview's right side on iPad and iPhone. At the end of one presentation, the next in the service follows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the screens">
    The clear buttons beside the preview take the words, media or anything else off. See [Looks, clearing and blackout](/presenting/looks-and-clearing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make your own

- **New Presentation…** (**⌘N** on the Mac) makes a presentation in the library that's picked, in the theme you choose.
- Type or paste a song's words into the **Flow** panel, and the slides follow. See [Songs](/building/songs).
- Drag presentations from a library into a playlist to build a service.
