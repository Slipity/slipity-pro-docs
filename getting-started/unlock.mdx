Every feature is in the free download. Build and share presentations, rehearse a service and try your screens. Until Slipity Pro is unlocked, a large Slipity watermark is laid over every screen, stream and recording.

## What the watermark covers

- Audience and stage screens, and Apple TVs running Slipity Screen
- Livestreams and recordings
- The preview while it's full screen

The small preview in the window doesn't show it, and nor do slides you export or share.

## Unlock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings › Slipity Pro">Or click **Remove Watermark** over the full-screen preview.</Step>
  <Step title="Click Unlock">The price is shown in your own currency. Confirm with the App Store.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="To capture: Settings › Slipity Pro with Unlock for its price and Restore Purchase.">
  <img src="/images/placeholder.svg" alt="Screenshot to come" />
</Frame>


One purchase unlocks Slipity Pro on every Mac, iPad and iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Restore a purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open **Settings › Slipity Pro** and click **Restore Purchase**.

<Note>If a purchase needs approval — Ask to Buy, or a bank — Slipity Pro says so and unlocks as soon as it's approved.</Note>
