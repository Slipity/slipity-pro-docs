Slipity Pro on iPad and iPhone has the same library, the same running order and the same editors as the Mac. What changes is how the window is laid out.

## iPad

Held sideways, iPad shows two columns, as the Mac does: the sidebar and preview in one, the slides in the other. Choose which corner the preview sits in under **Settings › Appearance › Preview Position**.

Held upright, it's one column, and the preview can sit at the **Top** or **Bottom**, be **Hidden**, or **Floating** in a corner you choose.

- **Apple Pencil** draws [Live Annotations](/presenting/live-annotations) on the preview.
- External displays and AirPlay act as audience or stage screens. See [Connecting screens](/getting-started/screens).

## iPhone

Upright, the preview sits at the top or bottom of the slides, or is hidden. Turned on its side, iPhone shows the preview and nothing else — the phone is being used to watch the output.

Pinch out on the preview on any iPad or iPhone to make it fill the screen, and pinch in to come back.

## iPhone Duo

- **Closed**, the outer screen is laid out like an iPhone, with the toolbar down the side beside the time and camera.
- **Open on its side**, the inner screen has the iPad's two columns, and **Preview Position** chooses the preview's corner.
- **Half open**, the fold divides the window: the slides on one half and the preview on the other, with nothing on the crease.
- **Open and upright**, it's laid out like an upright iPad.

The screen on the back of iPhone Duo can show your slides to whoever is facing you. Turn on **Back Screen Camera** from the **•••** menu, and a viewfinder in the corner shows what the back camera sees.

## Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

While a service runs on iPhone — or the iPhone is a remote for a Mac — a **Live Activity** shows the slide that's up, the presentation it's in and where you are in it, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

## Apple Watch

The Slipity Pro app on Apple Watch shows the slide that's up, large, with the next one below. **Tap** for the next slide; **swipe right** for the one before. It works through your iPhone, whether the iPhone is running the service or controlling a Mac.
