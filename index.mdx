Slipity Pro is worship presentation for Apple devices. Build a service once and run it from whichever device is in front of you: a Mac in the booth, an iPad on the music stand, or an iPhone in a small group.

## One library, every device

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mac" icon="laptop">The full production desk: projectors, stage screens and Apple TVs at once, recording and streaming, all from the keyboard.</Card>
  <Card title="iPad" icon="tablet">The same running order and editors, with Apple Pencil for Live Annotations.</Card>
  <Card title="iPhone" icon="mobile">Run a small group from your pocket, and follow along on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.</Card>
  <Card title="Apple TV" icon="tv">The free Slipity Screen app turns any TV into a screen, over your network.</Card>
</CardGroup>

Your library — presentations, themes, playlists and media — stays the same on every device that syncs it. See [Sync](/going-further/sync).
