---
title: "Settings reference"
description: "What's in each pane of Settings."
---

Open Settings with **⌘,** on the Mac, or from the toolbar's **•••** menu on iPad and iPhone. The search field at the top finds a setting by name.

| Pane | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Slipity Pro** | Unlock and Restore Purchase. See [Unlocking](/getting-started/unlock). |
| **Appearance** | Where the preview sits: a corner on the Mac and iPad, and on iPhone Duo open on its side; top, bottom, hidden or floating upright. |
| **Copyright & CCLI** | The copyright line, your CCLI licence number and the song usage report. |
| **Screens** | Audience and stage screens, displays, Apple TVs, calibration and test patterns. |
| **Audio & Video** | How foreground and background media play, and the audio meter. |
| **Inputs** | Cameras and capture cards, audio inputs, and what Dynamic Backgrounds listen to. |
| **Capture** | Recording and livestreaming. |
| **Themes** | Default themes, lines per slide, and blank slides at the start and end. |
| **Groups & Labels** | The groups and labels offered on every slide, and their colours. |
| **Bibles** | Translations: download or import. |
| **Search** | Search sources and their order. |
| **Voice Follow** | Live Scripture, Slide Navigation, Song Detection and what to listen to. |
| **Remote Control** | Remotes, MIDI and OSC. |
| **Integrations** | Planning Center and Canva. |
| **Sync** | iCloud or Google Drive. |
