---

Open Settings with **⌘,** on the Mac, or from the **•••** menu on iPad and iPhone. The search field at the top finds a setting by name, and opens its pane.

</Frame>

| Pane | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Slipity Pro** | Unlock and Restore Purchase. See [Unlocking](/getting-started/unlock). |
| **Appearance** | Where the preview sits: a corner on the Mac, iPad and iPhone Duo on its side; **Top**, **Bottom**, **Hidden** or **Floating** upright. **Live Annotations** (off to begin with), **Motion Blur** (on to begin with), **Live Activities** on iPhone and iPad, and on the Mac **Warn Before Quitting**. |
| **Copyright & CCLI** | The copyright line, your CCLI licence number, the copyright layout and the Song Usage Report. See [Copyright and CCLI](/going-further/copyright-and-ccli). |
| **Screens** | Audience and stage screens, displays and Apple TVs, Identify Screens, calibration and test patterns. See [Connecting screens](/getting-started/screens). |
| **Audio & Video** | How foreground and background media arrive, what audio does at the end, and the audio meter. See [Media](/building/media) and [Audio](/presenting/audio). |
| **Inputs** | Cameras and capture cards and their framing; audio inputs; what Dynamic Backgrounds listen to. See [Cameras](/presenting/cameras). |
| **Capture** | Recording and livestreaming. See [Recording and livestreaming](/going-further/recording-and-livestreaming). |
| **Themes** | Default themes for new songs, scripture and imports (scripture and lyrics take the theme on screen unless you choose otherwise); lines per slide; blank slides at the start and end. |
| **Groups & Labels** | The groups and labels offered on every slide, and their colours. |
| **Bibles** | Translations: download or import. |
| **Search** | Search sources, their order, and how lyrics found on the web are set. See [Search](/importing/search). |
| **Voice Follow** | Live Scripture, Slide Navigation, Song Detection, Web Lyrics Search, what to listen to, and the Glossary. See [Voice Follow](/presenting/voice-follow). |
| **Remote Control** | Controlling a Mac, an iPad or an iPhone from another. See [Remote control](/presenting/remote-control). |
| **MIDI & OSC** | Controllers and other apps: what a message does when it arrives, and what is sent when something happens. See [Remote control](/presenting/remote-control). |
| **Integrations** | Planning Center Services and LightLoop, which also connects Canva. |
| **Sync** | iCloud or Google Drive, media files, and on the Mac where media is kept. See [Sync](/going-further/sync). |

On the Mac, the open-source acknowledgments are in **Slipity Pro › About Slipity Pro**; on iPad and iPhone, under **Acknowledgments** in Settings.
