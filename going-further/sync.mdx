title: "Sync"
description: "Keep your library the same on every device."

Choose how your library syncs in **Settings › Sync**:

- **iCloud** — through every Mac and iPad signed in to your iCloud account, in your own private iCloud storage.
- **Google Drive** — for devices that don't share an iCloud account. Sign in to the same Google account (the church's) on each.
- **Off** — this device's library stays on this device.

## What syncs

Every library, with its presentations, themes, playlists, layouts, media lists and audio playlists — a library made on one device appears on the others. When the same thing is changed on two devices before they sync, the later change wins.

Screens, outputs, Looks and the CCLI usage report stay with each device.

<Note>With Google Drive, the library lives in a **Slipity Pro** folder in the account's Drive. If two devices first synced at the same moment, the Drive can end up with two such folders. Every device syncs with the oldest, and a device that had been using the other sends its whole library there the first time it does, so nothing is lost. **Settings › Sync** says when there's a second folder; once every device has opened Slipity Pro and synced, you can delete the newer one in Google Drive.</Note>

## Media files

Choose what each device keeps in **Download to This Mac** (or iPad, or iPhone):

- **Everything** — every file in the library.
- **What's Needed** — media in services changed or scheduled in the last month, every theme's and layout's, the audio playlists', and any Media panel list marked **Keep on This Mac**.
- **Only When Used** — nothing until it's used.

Whatever an open playlist uses is downloaded straight away, whichever you choose. To fetch one file sooner, use its download button in the [Media panel](/building/media#files-not-on-this-device).

## Where media is kept

On a Mac, media stays in the app's own folder unless you choose another. To keep it on an external drive or elsewhere, go to **Settings › Sync › Media Storage** and click **Choose Folder…**. This setting is there with sync off too.

Imported media and everything downloaded from iCloud or Google Drive go there. Files are kept in a **Slipity Pro Media** folder inside the one you pick. Your library itself stays on the Mac, and other devices choose for themselves.

Slipity Pro says how much it will copy and asks first. It copies and checks every file before removing the originals, and you can cancel partway with nothing changed. **Use Default** moves everything back.

<Warning>Use a drive formatted as APFS or exFAT. FAT32 can't hold files over 4 GB. Don't point two Macs at the same folder unless they sync the same library: each removes files its own library no longer uses.</Warning>

**If the drive isn't connected**, the library still opens, with a warning that its media is missing. Nothing downloads in the meantime, and anything you import waits on the Mac. Connect the drive and everything comes back without restarting, and what you imported moves across.
