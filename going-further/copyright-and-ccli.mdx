## The copyright line

Turn on **Enable Copyright Display** in **Settings › Copyright & CCLI**, enter your **CCLI License #**, and choose the **Copyright Display Location**.

What's shown comes from each song's own **copyright** box, in Edit under **Presentation** — and only for songs whose **Copyright** switch is on. The **CCLI Editor** (**Copyright Layout › Edit…**) arranges where the line sits and what it looks like.

## Song Usage Report

Slipity Pro keeps every song this device has put up, a day at a time, for your CCLI licence return. Open it with **Song Usage › Usage Report…**.

Each song has a dot — **amber** until it's been entered on the return, **green** once it has. Click a dot to change it, and work down the report a line at a time.

<Note>The report is kept on each device and isn't synced, so a church running services from two devices has a report on each.</Note>

The CCLI reports songs whether or not the copyright line is shown, so the report is kept either way.
