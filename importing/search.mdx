One search field looks through everything at once: your songs, scripture, your media — including the words inside your pictures — a lyrics site on the web, Planning Center and Canva.

On the Mac, press **⌘F**. While a service is running, search opens over the slides and keeps the preview in view, so the next thing can be found while this one is up.

## Choose and order the sources

In **Settings › Search › Sources**, switch sources on and off and drag them into the order you want results in. The search field says what it will search.
