One search field looks through everything at once: your songs, scripture, your media — including the words inside your pictures — a lyrics site on the web, Planning Center, LightLoop and Canva, and this help.

On the Mac, press **⌘F**. While a service is running, search opens over the slides and keeps the preview in view, so the next thing can be found while this one is up.

## Choose and order the sources

In **Settings › Search › Sources**, switch sources on and off and drag them into the order you want results in. The search field says what it will search.

## Ask for help

Type a question and choose its **Help** row. The answer opens beside Search, written from this help with the steps, pictures and videos that go with it. From there, **Open in Docs** goes to the page it came from, and **Talk to a Human** writes to us with your question in the email.

Help is last in the list of sources and on to begin with. Nothing is asked until you open the row.
