## ProPresenter

On the Mac, choose **File › Import › ProPresenter Files…** (**⇧⌘I**) and pick documents or a whole library. Groups, arrangements, themes and media come with them.

## Planning Center

Sign in under **Settings › Integrations › Planning Center Services**, then **Import a Plan** to bring in a service as a running order, or **Import Songs Only**.

## Canva

Sign in under **Settings › Integrations › Canva** and bring designs in from the Canva source in Search. Turn on **Import in 4K Where Available** for the sharpest result.

## Lyrics and other files

Drop plain text, ChordPro or OnSong files into a library. Each becomes a presentation, set in the theme for what it turns out to be: lyrics, a chord chart or sermon notes. A file whose title is already a presentation asks whether to replace it, bring it in beside it, or leave it out.

## Slipity files

Presentations shared from Slipity Pro open with a double-click, or with **File › Import › Slipity Files…** (**⌥⌘I**).
