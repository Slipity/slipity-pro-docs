## ProPresenter

On the Mac, choose **File › Import › ProPresenter Files…** (**⇧⌘I**), or drag ProPresenter documents, playlists or a whole library folder into the window. Groups, arrangements, themes and media come with them.

<Tip>A ProPresenter song often gives every slide its own numbered group. Pick all the slides and choose **Group › None** to take them off, or rename them.</Tip>

## Planning Center Services

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">In **Settings › Integrations › Planning Center Services**.</Step>
  <Step title="Import a Plan">Choose a plan. It arrives as a playlist, with its songs and their arrangements. **Import Songs Only** brings in the songs without the running order.</Step>
</Steps>

Rearrange the playlist, and the order goes back to the plan.

## Canva

Sign in under **Settings › Integrations › Canva**, then find designs through [Search](/importing/search). Turn on **Import in 4K Where Available** for the sharpest pictures.

## Lyrics, chord charts and notes

Drop plain text, ChordPro or OnSong files into a library, or paste them into [Flow](/building/songs#the-flow-editor). Each file becomes a presentation, set in the theme for what it turns out to be — lyrics, a chord chart, sermon notes or an OnSong file.

A file whose title is already a presentation asks whether to **replace** that presentation's words, bring it in **beside** it as another version, or **leave it out** — and, with more files to come, whether to do the same for all of them.

## Sermon notes

Notes in Word, RTF, HTML, PDF, Markdown or plain text can be read into slides. See [Sermon notes to slides](/building/sermon-notes).

## Bibles

Add translations in **Settings › Bibles**: **Download a Translation**, or **Import File**. See [Scripture](/building/scripture).

## Sharing with another church or Mac

Share presentations, themes and playlists as **Slipity files**, with the pictures, films and themes they need packed inside. Open one with a double-click, or **File › Import › Slipity Files…** (**⌥⌘I**). Something in the file that's already in your library is asked about first.

## Siri and Shortcuts

**Add Song to Service**, **Add Scripture to Service**, **Add Canva Design to Service** and **Add Pictures or Video to Service** build a service without opening the app. See [Siri and Shortcuts](/going-further/siri-and-shortcuts).
