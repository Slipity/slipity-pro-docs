## ProPresenter

On the Mac, choose **File › Import › ProPresenter Files…** (**⇧⌘I**), or drag ProPresenter documents, playlists or a whole library folder into the window. Groups, arrangements, themes and media come with them.

To bring in everything at once, choose **File › Import › ProPresenter Library…**. Slipity Pro finds the library on this Mac and asks what to bring in: presentations, playlists and themes. Presentations come into All Presentations, whatever ProPresenter library they were in. **Location** picks another folder if yours is kept elsewhere. A playlist brings its presentations with it, and ProPresenter's libraries become libraries of the same name. ProPresenter 6's `.pro6` files aren't supported.

## PowerPoint, Keynote and PDF

Choose **File › Import › PowerPoint or PDF…**, or drag a `.pptx` or `.pdf` onto All Presentations or a running order.

- **PowerPoint (.pptx)** comes in as slides you can edit: text boxes with their fonts, sizes, colors, bold and italic, alignment and bullets; pictures and films saved in the deck; backgrounds; shapes; groups; and SmartArt as the shapes it's drawn with. Speaker notes become each slide's notes, hidden slides come in disabled, and the deck's sections become sections. A deck of another shape than your slides — 4:3 on 16:9 — is placed in the middle at its own proportions, its background filling the slide.
- **PDF** comes in as a slide for each page, a picture of it, shown whole. Use it for **Keynote**, **Google Slides** and **Canva**: export the presentation as PDF, then import that. Keynote and Google Slides can also export PowerPoint, if you'd rather edit the words.

A few things don't come across, and the notice afterward says which: charts, tables (they come in as their words, a row to a line), animations and transitions, sounds, and films linked from elsewhere. Fonts that aren't on this Mac or iPad are shown in a close one where there is one, such as Helvetica Neue for Calibri; install the font and they show as designed.

PowerPoint's older `.ppt` files can't be read: open one in PowerPoint or Keynote, save it as `.pptx`, then import that.

A file whose name is already a presentation asks whether to **replace** it, bring it in as a **new version** beside it, or **skip** it.

## Planning Center Services

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">In **Settings › Integrations › Planning Center Services**.</Step>
  <Step title="Import a Plan">Choose a plan. It arrives as a playlist, with its songs and their arrangements. **Import Songs Only** brings in the songs without the running order.</Step>
</Steps>

Rearrange the playlist, and the order goes back to the plan.

## Canva

Canva connects through LightLoop. Sign in under **Settings › Integrations › LightLoop**, connect Canva there if it isn't already, then find designs through [Search](/importing/search). Turn on **Import in 4K Where Available** for the sharpest pictures.

## LightLoop

Connect your church's LightLoop under **Settings › Integrations › LightLoop**, and its media and playlists appear in [Search](/importing/search).

On the Mac, **Send to LightLoop**, in the **File** menu and beside **Export** in a presentation's or running order's menu, sends it to LightLoop for its Apple TVs.

## Lyrics, chord charts and notes

Drop plain text, ChordPro or OnSong files onto All Presentations, or paste them into [Flow](/building/songs#the-flow-editor). Each file becomes a presentation, set in the theme for what it turns out to be — lyrics, a chord chart, sermon notes or an OnSong file.

A file whose title is already a presentation asks whether to **replace** that presentation's words, bring it in **beside** it as another version, or **leave it out** — and, with more files to come, whether to do the same for all of them.

## Sermon notes

Notes in Word, RTF, HTML, PDF, Markdown or plain text can be read into slides. See [Sermon notes to slides](/building/sermon-notes).

## Bibles

Add translations in **Settings › Bibles**: **Download a Translation**, or **Import File**. See [Scripture](/building/scripture).

## Sharing with another church or Mac

Share presentations, themes, playlists, overlays, countdowns and stage layouts as **Slipity files**, with the pictures, films and themes they need packed inside. To write one, choose **File › Export › Slipity File…** (**⌥⌘E**), or **Export › Slipity File…** in a presentation's or playlist's menu. Pictures of the slides and printouts are in [Export and print](/importing/export-and-print). Open one with a double-click, or **File › Import › Slipity Files…** (**⌥⌘I**). Something in the file that's already in your library is asked about first: **Replace** it, or **Keep Both**.

In the Theme, Overlay, Countdown and Stage editors, right-click one in the list and choose **Export…**, or choose **Import…** from the **+** menu above the list. A presentation or playlist brings along the overlays, countdowns and stage layouts its slides use.

## Siri and Shortcuts

**Add Song to Service**, **Add Scripture to Service**, **Add Canva Design to Service** and **Add Pictures or Video to Service** build a service without opening the app. See [Siri and Shortcuts](/going-further/siri-and-shortcuts).
