---
title: "Export and Print"
description: "Write presentations and services out as Slipity files or as pictures, or print them as slides, rows with their words, or a lyric sheet for the band."
---

Choose **File › Export** with presentations picked in the list, or a playlist picked in the sidebar, or right-click one and choose **Export**:

- **Slipity File…** (**⌥⌘E**) packs it, with its themes, pictures and films, for another church or Mac. See [Sharing with another church or Mac](/importing/importing#sharing-with-another-church-or-mac).
- **Images…** writes a picture of each slide.
- **Print…** prints it. **File › Print…** (**⌘P**) does the same.

A playlist covers every presentation in the service, in order and in the arrangements the service uses. Only slides that are switched on are included.

## Images

Choose **PNG** or **JPEG**, and **1280 × 720**, **1920 × 1080** or **3840 × 2160**. Each presentation gets a folder of its own, with its slides numbered in order and named by their group, like *03 Chorus.png*. On the Mac, choose where the folders go; on iPhone and iPad, the share sheet offers **Save Images**, **Save to Files** and AirDrop.

Slipity Pro remembers the format and size for next time.

## Print

Pick a layout:

| Layout | What's on the page |
|---|---|
| **Grid** | The slides as pictures, 1 to 6 across (2 to start). |
| **Rows** | One slide per row, with its words beside it. |
| **Lyric Sheet** | The words alone, a group at a time, for the band or the singers. A group sung again is named, not written out again. |

Then choose what goes with them:

- **Title and page numbers** — the presentation's or service's name at the top of each page, and *Page 2 of 5* at the bottom.
- **Group labels** — each slide's group, such as *Verse 1* or *Chorus*, in its color.
- **Slide notes** — each slide's notes, in italics, under it.
- **Chords** (Lyric Sheet) — the chords set above the words where they fall.
- **Print on white** (Grid and Rows) — each slide as its words on white rather than as it looks on the screen, to save ink.

Pages are US Letter. Slipity Pro remembers your choices for the next print.

<Tip>To keep a PDF instead, choose **Save as PDF** in the Mac's print panel, or share from the print panel on iPhone and iPad.</Tip>
