## Groups

A **group** is a run of slides with a name and a colour: Verse 1, Chorus, Bridge. Right-click a slide and choose **Group** to start one there; it carries on until the next group starts. Pick several slides first to put just those slides in a group.

Set up the groups and colours offered on every slide in **Settings › Groups & Labels**.

## Labels

A **label** names one slide — "repeat", "instrumental", a note for whoever is at the desk. Right-click and choose **Label**.

## Arrangements

An **arrangement** is the order a song is sung in: Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Chorus, Chorus. Keep several for different services. The running order plays the arrangement, and a chorus sung three times is one set of slides, not three.

## Chords and transposing

Bring songs in with ChordPro or OnSong chords, show the chords to the band on a [stage screen](/presenting/stage-screens), and transpose the song into another key — with a capo where one helps.

## The Flow editor

To write or change a song's words quickly, use the Flow editor: type the lyrics with headings like `[Chorus]`, and the slides and groups follow the words.
