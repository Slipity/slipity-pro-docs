## Translations

Add Bibles in **Settings › Bibles**:

- **Download a Translation** from the list of those available.
- **Import File** to bring in a Bible you already have.

## Following along

With Voice Follow's **Live Scripture** on, saying a reference brings it up by itself. See [Voice Follow](/presenting/voice-follow).
