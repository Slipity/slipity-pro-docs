## Adding a passage to a presentation

The whole chapter shows, with the verses you searched for highlighted. **Add to Presentation** adds the highlighted verses, or the ones you've picked with **Command**-click, to the end of the presentation that's open.

They go on one slide, each verse with its number in superscript, so the room can follow where it is in the passage. Turn on **One Verse per Slide** above the button to give each verse a slide of its own, with its own reference. **Import** makes a new presentation of them, a verse a slide.

## Translations

Add Bibles in **Settings › Bibles**:

- **Get** one from the list of those available. If it can't be downloaded, you're told why.
- **Import Bible(s)** to bring in a Bible you already have. It's given a short code from its name, like NIV for NEW INTERNATIONAL VERSION.
- **Edit Code** beside a downloaded Bible changes its short code, which is what a slide's reference shows. Slides already made keep the reference they had.
