## Themes

A **theme** sets how a presentation's slides look: the fonts, the colours, the background and where the words go. A new library comes with three — for songs, scripture and sermons. Edit them, or make your own, in the **Theme Editor**.

Choose the theme songs, scripture and imported files get in **Settings › Themes**, along with **Lines per Slide**.

## The slide editor

Choose **Edit** and open a slide to work on its layers: text, shapes, pictures, films, cameras, countdowns and web pages. Anything reaching past the slide's edges is drawn faded, so you can find it and drag it back.

## Timelines

Every layer has a place on the slide's timeline: when it comes in, how — a fade, a move, a wipe — and when it goes. Take the slide live and the timeline plays on every screen.

## Other editors

- **Looks Editor** — which layers each screen shows. See [Looks](/presenting/looks-and-clearing).
- **Stage Editor** — stage screen layouts.
- **Overlay Editor** — lower thirds, logos and anything that sits over the slides.
- **Countdown Editor** — countdowns and timers.
- **CCLI Editor** — where the copyright line sits and how it looks.
