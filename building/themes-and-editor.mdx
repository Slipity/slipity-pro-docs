## Themes

A **theme** sets how a presentation's slides look: fonts, colours, background and where the words go. A new library comes with three — for songs, scripture and sermons. Edit them, or make your own, in the **Theme Editor**.

- Choose a presentation's theme from **Themes** in the toolbar, or in its inspector.
- **New Theme from Selection…** on a slide's right-click menu makes a theme from how that slide looks.
- Choose the themes new songs, scripture and imported files get in **Settings › Themes**.

## The slide editor

In **Edit**, open a slide to work on its layers. The editor has:

- the **slides** of the presentation down one side,
- the **canvas**, where layers are moved, resized, turned and snapped to each other and the edges,
- the **layer list**,
- the **inspector**, with the slide's settings and the picked layer's,
- the **timeline** below.

Anything reaching past the slide's edges is drawn faded, so it can be found and dragged back.

## Layers

| Layer | |
|---|---|
| **Text** | Words, with full formatting. Chords can be drawn over them. |
| **Scripture** | A passage and its reference. |
| **Media** | A picture or film. Pictures have [Image Tools](/building/media#image-tools). |
| **Camera** | A live camera, with its own [framing](/presenting/cameras). |
| **YouTube** | A YouTube video, streamed on the screens. |
| **Web page** | A webpage, shown as a picture and refreshed as often as you choose. |
| **Timer** | A [countdown or elapsed time](/presenting/countdowns-and-timers). |
| **Shape** | Rectangles, circles and more, with fills and borders. |
| **QR code** | Drawn sharp at any size, with the margin it needs to scan. |
| **Translation** | The words in another language. See [Translation](/building/translation). |
| **Copyright** | The song's copyright line. |
| **Group** | Layers kept together, to move and animate as one. |
| **Annotations** | Lines drawn live. See [Live Annotations](/presenting/live-annotations). |

A layer can be shown only when another has words — a reference box that disappears on slides without a reference.

## Timelines

Every layer has a place on the slide's timeline: when it comes in, how — fades, moves, wipes, with curves — and when it goes. Films and sounds are clips with in and out points and transitions between them. Take the slide live and its timeline plays on every screen, in step.

| Key | |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Play and pause |
| **⌘T** | Split a clip at the playhead |
| **⌥⌘[**, **⌥⌘]**, **⌥⌘T** | A transition at the start, end or both ends of a clip |
| **← →** | Move the playhead a frame; with **Shift**, ten |

**Markers** on the timeline can do something when they're reached.

## Export a slide as a film

On the Mac, choose **File › Export Slide as Movie…** (**⇧⌘E**) to write the slide you're editing — timeline, films and sound — out as a film for social media or another venue's screens.

## Other editors

- **Looks Editor** — what each screen shows. See [Looks](/presenting/looks-and-clearing).
- **Stage Editor** — stage screen layouts.
- **Overlay Editor** — lower thirds, logos and anything over the slides.
- **Countdown Editor** — countdowns.
- **CCLI Editor** — where the copyright line sits and how it looks.
