The **Media** panel holds your pictures, films and audio. Drag one onto a slide to put it behind the words, or after a slide as a **Media item** that plays on its own.

## Behind a slide or after it

Right-click a slide and choose **Add Media…**:

- **Behind This Slide** puts a picture or film behind the words.
- **As Media After This Slide** adds a Media item that plays on the Media layer.
- **YouTube Video After This Slide…** adds a YouTube video.

**Remove Background Media** takes it off again.

## Dynamic Backgrounds

Motion backgrounds that move with the music: what's playing, or a live input from the band. Find them in the **Dynamic Backgrounds** section of the Media panel, and choose what they listen to in **Settings › Inputs**.

## Image Tools

Select a picture on a slide and use:

- **Remove Background** to lift a person or object off its background.
- **Clean Up** to brush away what shouldn't be in the picture.

Both work on your device, and the original stays in the Media panel.

## Smart Media

Every picture and film you add is read on your device: tagged by what it shows, and read for any words written on it. Search for "tent" or for the title printed on a sermon series graphic, and it's found.
