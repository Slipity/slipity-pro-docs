The **Set Builder** suggests songs for a service, and puts them in a playlist.

  <img src="/images/placeholder.svg" alt="Screenshot to come" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what the sermon is about">Type a topic or a passage — "Living water", "John 4:7–26" — or choose **Or read the sermon** to let it read a presentation of sermon notes. It says what it read the sermon as.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to weigh them">**Prefer songs the church sings often**, and **Order them to flow: upbeat to reflective, keys close together**.</Step>
  <Step title="Find Songs">It weighs every song by its topics, its words, how well the church knows it and how they'd flow together.</Step>
  <Step title="Make the playlist">Name it and put the set in. Change anything you like afterwards — it's an ordinary playlist.</Step>
</Steps>

## Topics

Every song is read for what it's about, against your library's list of **Topics**. Choose **Edit Topics…** to add, rename or remove them, and **Let the Mac Read Them** to read songs again. A song whose topics you've changed by hand keeps them.

<Note>Reading songs uses Apple Intelligence on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.</Note>
