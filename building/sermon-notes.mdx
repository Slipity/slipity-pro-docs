---
title: "Sermon notes to slides"
description: "Paste the notes in, and get a presentation of points, scripture and quotes."
---

Paste or open a sermon's notes, and Apple Intelligence reads them and proposes slides: the points, the scripture, and quotes with who said them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in the notes">Paste them, type them, or open a document — Word, RTF, HTML, PDF, Markdown or plain text.</Step>
  <Step title="Look over the slides">Every proposed slide can be changed or taken out.</Step>
  <Step title="Make the presentation">It's set in your sermon theme, ready to go.</Step>
</Steps>
