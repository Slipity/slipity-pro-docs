A **translation layer** shows a slide's words in another language: under the English for a bilingual congregation, or on its own on a screen for one language group.

## Add one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the slide or theme in the editor">Add a layer and choose **Translation**. Put it in a theme to translate every slide in that theme.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the language">And whose words it translates — normally **The Slide's Words**.</Step>
  <Step title="Style it">Like any text layer: smaller, in italics, a different colour.</Step>
</Steps>

## Where the words come from

- **Scripture** comes from a Bible in that language, when the slide's reference can be read and the translation is in **Settings › Bibles**.
- **Everything else** is translated on your device, line by line, so a lyric keeps its lines.

Translations are made ahead of time, whenever the words change, so a slide shows its translation the moment it goes up — on any screen, with nothing to wait for. The layer's settings say where each translation came from, and let you correct one by hand.
