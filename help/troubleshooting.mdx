---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "When a screen, an Apple TV or a purchase doesn't behave."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A projector or TV isn't showing anything">
    Check that the screen is on in the **Screens** menu (**⌥⌘1** for audience screens), then choose **Refresh Displays** (**⇧⌘R**). In **Settings › Screens**, make sure the display is chosen for a screen, and use **Identify Screens** to see which is which.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No Apple TV appears">
    The Apple TV and this device must be on the same network, with Slipity Screen open on the TV. On the Mac, check that Slipity Pro may use the local network in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An Apple TV says it's in use by another Mac">
    Pair it again from this device. Pairing takes over from any Mac driving it now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The watermark is still showing after buying">
    Open **Settings › Slipity Pro** and click **Restore Purchase**, signed in to the Apple Account you bought with. A purchase waiting for approval unlocks as soon as it's approved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voice Follow isn't hearing anything">
    Check **Listen To** in **Settings › Voice Follow**, and that Slipity Pro may use the microphone and speech recognition in **System Settings › Privacy & Security**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A change on one device hasn't reached another">
    Both devices need the same sync in **Settings › Sync** — the same iCloud account, or the same Google account. Screens, outputs and Looks stay with each device on purpose.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
