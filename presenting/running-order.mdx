The sidebar holds your **libraries**, where presentations are kept, and your **playlists**, which put them in the order of a service. The middle of the window shows the slides of what's picked, in a grid or a list.

## Moving through a service

- Click a slide in Play to put it up.
- The arrow keys step through slides, and on into the next presentation at the end of one.
- Give a slide a **Hot Key** — a single character — to bring it up from anywhere with one key.

## Picking several slides

**Shift**-click picks a run of slides, **Command**-click adds and removes single ones, and dragging across the grid picks everything it passes over. Picked slides are outlined in blue.

## Right-click a slide

The menu acts on the slide you clicked, or on every picked slide when that slide is one of them. While the menu is open, the slides it's about are outlined.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Add Slide** | A new slide in the presentation's theme, after this one. |
| **Quick Edit**, **Edit Slide** | Change the words where they are, or open the editor. |
| **Enable / Disable** | Take a slide out of the running order for one service without deleting it. |
| **Hot Key…** | One key that brings this slide up. |
| **Go to Next Timer…** | Move on by itself after a number of seconds. |
| **Add Media…** | A picture or film behind this slide, or as a Media item after it. |
| **Add Action** | Things that happen when the slide goes live: MIDI, OSC, a webhook, an overlay, a countdown, an audio playlist, a Look, a camera, a stage layout, recording or streaming. |
| **Clear** | Take layers off the screens when the slide goes live. |
| **Group**, **Label** | Name a run of slides — Verse 1, Chorus — or one slide. |
| **Copy**, **Cut**, **Paste**, **Delete** | Slides move with everything on them: their group, label, actions and media. |
