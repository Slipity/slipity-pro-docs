The **Audio** layer plays music under whatever is on the screens. It doesn't follow the running order: pre-service music starts before the service, and a bed under a prayer carries on while the slides change above it.

Open the **Audio** panel from the toolbar.

## Playlists

Make audio playlists of tracks, and play one from the panel, from a slide (**Add Action › Play Audio Playlist**), or with Siri and Shortcuts. Tracks cross-fade into each other. Click a playlist to open it; click it again to rename it.

A slide that plays the playlist already playing lets it carry on, and picks it up where it was paused. So the first slide of an announcement loop can start the music without cutting it back to the beginning every time the loop comes round.

Each track shows who it's by, more faintly, beside its name: the artist you gave it, or else the one in the file. The button beside **+** above the tracks switches between the list and a grid of covers, with each track's name and artist under its cover. Click a cover to play it, as you would a row. On iPhone and iPad, pinch the grid to make the covers bigger or smaller; its size is kept apart from the slide grid's.

**All Audio**, above the playlists, lists every track in your library, whether or not it's in a playlist. Click one to play it, or choose **Add to Playlist** from its menu. Files dropped on All Audio come into the library without going into a playlist.

On iPad and iPhone the music keeps playing with the screen locked or another app in front. What's playing shows on the Lock Screen and in Control Center, where play, pause, next, previous and the scrubber work as they do in the panel, and so do the buttons on headphones.

**Smart Mix**, the switch between Shuffle and Repeat, plays a playlist the way a DJ would. It starts on a song picked at random from those already analysed, unless a song is already playing or you start from one, and each song is followed by the one closest to it in key and tempo, counting half and double time, and the change starts on a phrase near the end of the song and blends over about eight bars, with the next song's first full bar coming in exactly on the outgoing song's beat. It works from what Slipity heard when it analysed each song; songs not analysed yet play after the rest, in the playlist's order, with an ordinary blend. When two songs are within about 6% of each other's tempo, counting half and double time, the first is eased gradually to the next one's tempo over the sixteen bars before the blend, at its own pitch, and kept on the next song's beat through the blend; the next song plays at its own tempo. Songs further apart than that blend at their own tempos.

What a track does when it finishes is set in **Settings › Audio & Video › On Reaching the End**: **stop**, **play again**, or go on to the **next** in its list. Next is what pre-service music wants. A single track can be told otherwise from its own menu.

## A track's name, artist and cover

Choose **Edit Info…** from a track's menu to change its title, who it's by, and its cover: choose a picture, paste one, drop one on the square, or remove it. Leave the title or artist empty to use what the file says. Double-click a track's name to rename it in place, and you can also drop a picture on the track, or on the cover of what's playing.

None of it changes the file. A track renamed in one list is renamed in every list, and in the slide actions that play it.

## Now Playing on the screens

Before and after a service, someone always asks what song is playing. In the **Overlay Editor**, add a **Now Playing** layer: a card with the song's cover, title and artist, and a bar showing how far through it is. Choose a **Pill**, **Rounded** or **Rectangle** card, its colour, and whether it shows the artwork, artist and progress bar; its words take the layer's font and colour from the Text tab. Put the overlay up and it follows the music, and shows nothing while the music is paused or stopped.

## The playing song

Open the playing song large to:

- **scrub** through it,
- **jump between its sections** — while it plays, clicking a section is a cue: the song finishes the section it's in and goes on from the one you clicked, the way a band follows a leader,
- name, split and join sections while it's stopped,
- play it in another **arrangement**: its sections in another order, dragged about, repeated or taken out, without touching the file,
- write that arrangement out with **Export as .m4a…**, with its name and cover.

## The audio meter

A stereo meter on the preview measures everything the room hears: music, slide soundtracks and films, after each one's volume and mute. **Display Audio Meter on Preview** in **Settings › Appearance** chooses when it's there: **Auto** (the default) slides it in while anything can be heard and away a few seconds after the room falls quiet, **Show** keeps it up, and **Hide** turns it off. **Show Decibel Marks** adds a scale.

## Lyric videos

A presentation can play a track and put its slides up at moments in it. See [Slide actions › Lyric videos](/presenting/slide-actions#lyric-videos).
