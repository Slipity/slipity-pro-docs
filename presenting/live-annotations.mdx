Circle a word, underline a verse, sketch a map. With Apple Pencil, a finger or the pointer, every line appears on the screens as you draw it.

## Draw

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the pencil">It's over the preview while a slide is up.</Step>
  <Step title="Draw on the preview">Choose a colour, and **Pen** for writing or **Marker** for underlining. While you draw, a tap or swipe on the preview draws rather than changing the slide.</Step>
  <Step title="Click Done">Or press **Escape**.</Step>
</Steps>

**Undo** takes back the last line. **Clear** takes off everything drawn since you started.

## Kept with the slide

When you finish, or move to another slide, your lines stay on the slide they were drawn on, as an **Annotations** layer. They're the strokes themselves, not a picture of them, so they stay sharp at any size and can be edited like any other layer.

To take them off, use **Clear Annotations** beside the pencil. It can be undone.
