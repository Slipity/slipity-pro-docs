title: "Stage screens"
description: "Show the people on the platform what's next, the time, notes and chords."
---

A stage screen shows a **stage layout** instead of what the audience sees. A layout can hold:

- the **current** and **next** slide,
- a **clock** and [timers](/presenting/countdowns-and-timers),
- the slide's **notes**,
- **chords** for the band, in the song's key,
- the **stage message**,
- anything else a slide can hold.

## Set one up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give a display the Stage role">In **Settings › Screens**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose its layout">Each stage screen has its own **Stage Layout**.</Step>
  <Step title="Design layouts">In the **Stage Editor**, starting from one of the layouts there or from nothing.</Step>
</Steps>

## Changing layout during a service

A slide can change a stage screen's layout when it goes live — chords for the band's songs, notes and a clock for the sermon. Right-click the slide and choose **Add Action › Stage**, then the screen and its layout. Siri and Shortcuts can do it too (**Show Stage Layout**).

## Stage messages

Put a line of text in front of the people on the platform:

- On the Mac, **Screens › Send Stage Message…** (**⌥⌘M**), and **Clear Stage Message** to take it down.
- From a remote, or Siri and Shortcuts (**Send Stage Message**, **Clear Stage Message**).

## Notes

Each slide has **notes**, shown on stage screens and never to the audience. Add them in the slide inspector, or let [Sermon notes](/building/sermon-notes) fill them in.
