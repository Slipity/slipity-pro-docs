A stage screen shows a **stage layout**, not what the audience sees: the current and next slide, a clock, the speaker's notes, chords for the band, a timer, or a message.

## Set one up

1. In **Settings › Screens**, give a display the **Stage** role.
2. Choose its **Stage Layout**.
3. Design layouts in the **Stage Editor**.

A slide can change what a stage screen shows when it goes live: right-click it and choose **Add Action › Stage Layout** — chords for the band's song, notes and a clock for the sermon.

## Stage messages

On the Mac, choose **Screens › Send Stage Message…** (**⌥⌘M**) to put a line of text on the stage screens, and **Clear Stage Message** to take it down.
