Voice Follow listens through a microphone or an audio input and follows along. Turn each part on with its toolbar button.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Follow Scripture** (Live Scripture) | Say a reference — "John three sixteen" — or start reading, and the verse comes up. A verse it put up can come down by itself after a while; choose how long in **Take a Verse Down After**. |
| **Follow Slides** (Slide Navigation) | As a song is sung or a script is read, the next slide comes up when the words do. |
| **Shazam Song Detection** | When the band starts a song, it's recognised and found in your library. |
| **Web Lyrics Search** | A recognised song that isn't in your library can be found on the web. |

## Set it up

In **Settings › Voice Follow**:

- **Listen To** — a microphone, or a channel of an audio interface. The sound desk's feed is cleaner than a microphone in the room.
- **Words to Match** — how many words must match before it moves.
- Which follow buttons appear in the toolbar, and what each does when it hears something: **Suggest** shows what it found in a banner to confirm, **Display** puts it up, and **Do Nothing** only listens, for the transcript and live captions.

Scripture placed by Voice Follow goes on the **Overlays** layer, over the slides, so it doesn't replace what's already up.

## Live captions

Put what's being said on the screens as it's said. In the **Overlay Editor**, add a **Live Captions** layer. It starts along the bottom, with room for two lines; style and place it like any other layer, then put the overlay up.

Choose its **Language**: **As Spoken**, or another language to translate into. A language may need downloading first, and the layer offers that. Translation happens on your device a moment after the words are said. Machine translation can be wrong, so have someone check it before relying on it.

For two languages at once, add a second Live Captions layer.

<Tip>To transcribe and caption without Voice Follow moving anything on the screens, set Live Scripture and Slide Navigation to **Do Nothing**.</Tip>

## Glossary

Corrections apply to everything transcribed, for Voice Follow and live captions alike, whatever case they're heard in. In **Settings › Voice Follow › Glossary**:

- **Common Biblical and Theological Phrases** puts right the names and phrases a transcriber usually gets wrong: "Thessalonians", "Habakkuk", "Lamb of God".
- **Add Correction** adds your own. Good ones to add are staff names, ministries, website addresses and social handles: **Heard** "Pastor Kurt", **Written** "Pastor Curt".

You can also correct as you go. In the **Transcript**, pick the words and choose **Correct**. They're put right there, and whenever they're heard again. Corrected words show in blue in the transcript.

<Note>Speech is turned into words on your device and never leaves it.</Note>
