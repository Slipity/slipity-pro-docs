title: "Voice Follow"

---

Voice Follow listens through a microphone or an audio input and follows along.

- **Live Scripture**: say a reference, or start reading, and the verse comes up.
- **Slide Navigation**: as a song is sung or notes are read, the next slide comes up when the words do.
- **Shazam Song Detection**: when the band starts a song, it's found in your library.
- **Web Lyrics Search**: a song that isn't in your library can be found on the web.

## Set it up

In **Settings › Voice Follow**, choose what to **Listen To** — a microphone, or a channel of your audio interface — and which of the follow buttons appear in the toolbar.

<Note>Speech is turned into words on your device and never leaves it.</Note>
