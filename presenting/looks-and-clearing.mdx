What's on the screens is built from layers:

| Layer | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Media** | Backgrounds, pictures and films |
| **Video Input** | A live camera or capture card |
| **Slides** | Lyrics, scripture and everything on a slide |
| **Overlays** | Lower thirds, logos, and scripture placed by Voice Follow |
| **Countdown** | Countdown banners and timers |
| **Audio** | Backing tracks and soundtracks |

## Clearing

The clear buttons take one layer off the screens — **Clear Slides**, **Clear Media**, and so on — with the service's transition, so the words fade off the way they came on. A slide can clear layers by itself when it goes live: right-click it and choose **Clear**.

## Looks

A **Look** decides which layers a screen shows and how. For example, a Look can keep the lobby TV on the countdown while the auditorium shows the lyrics, or set the words in another theme for the livestream.

Make and change Looks in the **Looks Editor**, and switch the live Look from the **Screens** menu on the Mac or from a slide's **Add Action › Look**.
