## From another device

Any Mac, iPad or iPhone running Slipity Pro can control another on the same network — a laptop in the booth running the Mac that drives the projectors, an iPhone in your hand running an iPad on the stage, or one iPhone running another. Where there is no network, they connect over Wi-Fi straight between the two.

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the device running the service">Open **Settings › Remote Control** and turn on **Allow Remote Control**.</Step>
  <Step title="On the remote">Turn on **Remote Control**. Macs, iPads and iPhones that allow it appear under **Devices**. Choose **Pair…**.</Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">The device being controlled shows a code. Type it on the remote.</Step>
  <Step title="Take control">Choose **Control**. **Stop Controlling** hands it back.</Step>
</Steps>

The remote shows the other device's running order with pictures of its slides and what it's showing now, and steps through the service from your hand. The remote's own screens are off meanwhile. The device being controlled shows who is controlling it.

- **Forget** removes one device from a remote; **Forget All** on the device being controlled removes every paired remote.
- A remote's Apple Watch and Live Activity follow the device it controls too. See [iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo](/getting-started/iphone-and-ipad).

### Controlling an iPad or iPhone

An iPad or iPhone can be controlled only while Slipity Pro is open and in front on it: iOS stops an app listening on the network in the background. While a remote is connected it doesn't lock by itself. If you switch away, remotes show it as not on the network, and connect again on their own when you come back to Slipity Pro.

An iPhone that is also driving screens draws smaller pictures of its slides for remotes, so the screens come first.

## MIDI

In **Settings › MIDI & OSC › MIDI**, map notes and messages from a controller to commands — next slide, previous slide, clear, blackout, a Look — and choose what Slipity Pro **Sends**. A slide can send MIDI when it goes live with **Add Action › Communication › MIDI…**.

## OSC

In **Settings › MIDI & OSC › OSC**, turn on **Enable OSC Server** and choose a **UDP Port** to let a lighting desk, show controller or companion app drive Slipity Pro. Add places to **Send To** for OSC going the other way, and send OSC from a slide with **Add Action › Communication › OSC…**.
