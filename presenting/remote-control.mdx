## From iPhone or iPad

Any iPhone or iPad running Slipity Pro can control a Mac on the same network.

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Mac">Open **Settings › Remote Control** and turn on **Allow Remote Control**.</Step>
  <Step title="On the iPhone or iPad">Turn on **Remote Control**, find the Mac in the list and choose **Pair…**.</Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">The Mac shows a code. Type it on the remote. After that, choose **Control** to take over.</Step>
</Steps>

The remote shows the Mac's running order and what it's showing, and steps through the service from your hand.

## MIDI

In **Settings › Remote Control**, set MIDI notes and messages to commands — next slide, clear, a Look — and send MIDI from a slide with **Add Action › Communication › MIDI…**.

## OSC

Turn on **Enable OSC Server** and choose a **UDP Port** to let a lighting desk or show controller drive Slipity Pro. Slides can send OSC to other equipment when they go live.
